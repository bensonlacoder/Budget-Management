## Entering a purchase

1. Choose the venue or Marketplace station and the dish. A pasta shape, a burger patty or a splash of syrup belongs with the dish. A separately sold side can also be recorded on its own.
2. Select the portion, drink size or paid extras that match the order. For example, a half portion of pasta with chicken is 8.25 + 3.10 = **11.35 points**. The full portion with chicken and shrimp is 11.90 + 3.10 + 3.10 = **18.10 points**. [Trattoria’s portion note and protein prices](https://scudining.cafebonappetit.com/cafe/marketplace-2/2026-11-20/)
3. Review the total before saving. Where the operator supplies no price, use the points charged on the receipt or in Transact. A student with only the receipt total should be able to record that amount in one entry. SCU values one dining point at one dollar and deducts the actual purchase amount from the balance. [SCU dining plans](https://www.scu.edu/auxiliary-services/dining-services/dining-plans/)
